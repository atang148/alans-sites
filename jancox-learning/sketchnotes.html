The Glossary 191 terms

The vocabulary. Every term names a piece of the machinery — The Act, the City, the circuits, the Aim — so what runs a man automatically can be seen, and talked about, precisely.

The Code of Conduct 157 maxims

The practice. The Revolutionist's code — how the certain man actually behaves, day to day, while the machinery runs everyone else. Short rules, lived, not debated.

e.g. “Don't steal or be deceitful.” · “Stay out of debt.” · “You should have no interest in other people's personal lives.”

The Acronyms 99

The shorthand. The system compressed into letter-codes — pocket reminders of whole ideas, each one unpacking into a glossary definition when you stop and expand it.

✎ the system at a glance

1

Man is mechanical

Asleep in The Act: under normal conditions man does nothing — everything is done through him, a cog in a living machine.

2

Life uses man

Life holds all the pieces — men are its cells and pawns, moved forward toward Life's own aim of constant expansion.

3

Seeing it: neuralizing

The few begin to watch the machine — thinking about a thing other than native consciousness would, instead of being thought by it.

4

The Code as practice

The Aim, lived: 157 maxims turn seeing into conduct — doing and saying what you are not by temperament inclined to do.

✎ six key concepts — in the glossary's own words

The ACT

Ordinary life as a staged play: no one knows what thought they will next have, yet the collective life of humanity proceeds as though thoroughly scripted.

— glossary definition

MECHANICAL

The ordinary state itself: that which is mechanical is that which is ordinary — everything done through a man, not by him.

— glossary definition

The CITY

The inner landscape of ordinary life, where the pail one is given is never filled — and where people will do anything except what works.

— glossary definition

MAPS

Every description of the teaching — this page included: two dimensional descriptions of a three-dimensional reality; temporary, ever-changing, ultimately disposable.

— glossary definition

NEURALIZING

The core exercise: thinking about something in a manner other than your native consciousness normally would — floating a thought above the grasp of either-true-or-false.

— glossary definition

The AIM

What the whole activity is for: to make consciousness conscious of itself and its heritage — and to entertain only thoughts original with your own consciousness.

— glossary definition
