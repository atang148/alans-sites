stay safe

romance scams target older daters hard — adults 60+ lost billions to fraud in recent years, hundreds of millions to romance scams alone (FBI). rules that never bend:
· never send money, gift cards, or financial info to someone you met online, no matter the story.
· video call before meeting in person — scammers dodge cameras.
· first dates in busy public places (coffee shops, restaurants); tell a friend or family member who you're meeting and where.
· walk away from anyone rushing toward love, refusing video, or with a story that keeps changing.

getting started

1. pick one low-pressure thing this week: a senior center lunch, an OLLI lecture, or a meetup dinner.
2. show up twice — familiar faces turn into conversations.
3. if you want dating too, try one app (ourtime for casual, silversingles for serious) with a recent, smiling photo and one hobby photo.
4. keep the safety rules above taped to the fridge. seriously.
